## Keep exactly
- Every person label (man_1, woman_1, boy_1, girl_1, person_1) exactly as written in the fields: lowercase, underscore, number, no brackets or quotes. Every label in the fields appears in the prompt at least once. Never invent a label or rename one.

## Shorten in this order

## Form
Plain prose sentences only: no headings, no field names, no lists, no line breaks, no quotation of these rules, no commentary before or after the prompt. Never add content missing from the fields. Leave out any category whose field is empty rather than inventing it.
