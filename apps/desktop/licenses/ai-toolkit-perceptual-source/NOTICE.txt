SceneWorks implements the referenced body-loss architectures and formulas natively in Rust:
toolkit/body_id.py -> ViTPose+ keypoints, visibility and bone-length proportions;
toolkit/body_shape.py -> HybrIK ResNet-34 beta-shape head and person-crop comparison;
toolkit/normal_id.py -> Sapiens normal encoder/decoder and normal comparison.
The Candle and MLX implementations use their native tensor/autograd operations. Their documented
adaptations include a shared reference-time ViTPose person gate, subject-mask geometry alignment,
and decoded-clean-x0 reference tensors. The AB source/reference producer records these differences.

This entry covers source provenance and the MIT source grant only. Optional model checkpoints
retain their own provider terms. No permission to redistribute model weights is asserted here.
The upstream THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md separately identifies an Apache-2.0 Rose optimizer, which is
not implemented or linked by these four crates; this body-source entry does not cover Rose.
