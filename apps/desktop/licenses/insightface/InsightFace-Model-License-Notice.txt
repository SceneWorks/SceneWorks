InsightFace Model License Notice
================================

Applies to the SCRFD face detector (scrfd_10g) and the ArcFace face recognition
embedder (arcface_iresnet100) used by SceneWorks' face-analysis features. These
models originate from the InsightFace project (deepinsight/insightface).

InsightFace publishes no standalone license document for its model weights; the
governing terms are stated in the project README (License section,
https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface). The upstream statements are
reproduced verbatim below:

  "The code of InsightFace is released under the MIT License. There is no
  limitation for both academic and commercial usage."

  "The training data containing the annotation (and the models trained with
  these data) are available for non-commercial research purposes only."

  "Both manual-downloading models from our github repo and auto-downloading
  models with our python-library follow the above license policy(which is for
  non-commercial research purposes only)."

Summary of effect: the InsightFace CODE license (MIT) does not govern these
weights. The SCRFD and ArcFace model weights are trained on InsightFace's
annotated training data and are therefore available for NON-COMMERCIAL RESEARCH
PURPOSES ONLY under the upstream terms above. Anyone requiring commercial use
of these models must obtain a separate license from the InsightFace team
(contact@insightface.ai).

Provenance: SceneWorks stages these weights as safetensors conversions
(SceneWorks/instantid-mlx) of the InsightFace-published model files. Conversion
does not change the governing terms; the converted weights remain derivatives
of the upstream models and carry the same non-commercial-research restriction.
